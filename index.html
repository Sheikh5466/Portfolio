About Me

Turning repetitive work into systems that run themselves

FAQ

Questions clients ask first

How long does a typical automation take to build?+

A focused chatbot or workflow usually takes a few days from our discovery call. Larger systems — like a full lead qualification pipeline or an AI receptionist — take one to two weeks, including testing. You get a clear timeline before we start, and I stick to it.

Which tools and platforms do you work with?+

My core stack is n8n, Make and Zapier for workflows, OpenAI GPT models for the AI layer, and the WhatsApp Business API, Telegram, Twilio, Google Sheets, Airtable and most CRMs for integrations. If your tool has an API or a webhook, I can almost certainly connect it.

Do you need access to my accounts?+

Only the specific tools the automation touches, and only with the access level needed. I’ll tell you exactly what I need and why before we begin — and you can revoke access any time after launch.

What does an automation project cost?+

Every project is quoted as a fixed price after a free discovery call, based on scope — no hourly surprises. A simple chatbot costs far less than a multi-system pipeline, and I’ll always tell you honestly if a smaller build solves your problem.

What happens after launch — can you maintain it?+

Yes. Every launch includes a walkthrough and documentation so you understand your system. If you’d rather not think about it again, I offer ongoing support — monitoring, fixes and improvements as your business grows.
